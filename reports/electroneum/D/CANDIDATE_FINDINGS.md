## D-CAND-001 — Receipt-delivery queue synchronization omission
- **Status:** DISPROVEN (2026-11-04 01:45 NPT): the alleged missing mutex is present in the exact immutable certified source. This candidate was a false positive caused by an incorrect prior source reading; no race reproducer was executed.
- **Source:** certified immutable Windeep commit `7eb84ee39bd01eaaf1f64afbd871b06c85ff3bb0`, `eth/downloader/queue.go:805-826` `(*queue).DeliverReceipts`, `:831-904` `(*queue).deliver`, compared with `:769-772` `DeliverBodies` and `:907-915` `Prepare`.
- **Observed, corrected by immutable-source reread:** `DeliverReceipts` **does** execute `q.lock.Lock()` and `defer q.lock.Unlock()` immediately before building its validator and calling `q.deliver`; `DeliverBodies` likewise holds the lock. The prior claim that the lock was absent was false. `deliver` explicitly documents that the caller MUST hold the queue write lock; it reads/deletes `pendPool[id]`, mutates task queues/result slots and signals active results. `Prepare` locks the same mutex.
- **Attacker prerequisites/reachability:** irrelevant to this rejected missing-lock allegation; the mutex guards the call irrespective of peer response timing. No peer-controlled historical rewrite demonstrated.
- **Impact:** none established for this disproven allegation. The hypothesized unguarded pending-map access does not occur in the inspected function. Availability effects may warrant class-B handoff after confirmation; class-D ownership remains receipt ingestion integrity.
- **Decisive counterexample:** immutable certified commit `7eb84ee39bd01eaaf1f64afbd871b06c85ff3bb0`, `eth/downloader/queue.go:805-826` contains `q.lock.Lock()` followed by `defer q.lock.Unlock()`; both precede `q.deliver`. GitHub file blob SHA `5eb0e1f526afeeb84b382430d450a23c62d07740` matches branch and immutable-object reads.
- **Confidence:** high that the specific missing-lock allegation is false; no broader claim of race freedom.
- **Next action:** close this candidate and rotate to packet-derived receipt hash integrity and authenticated header binding. A race test would require a distinct concrete interleaving hypothesis; adding an already-present mutex is not a meaningful negative control.

Add a scratch guard mutant to prove the harness detects altered pivot acceptance. Two variants: head just above `fsMinFullBlocks` and a deeper head with competing sibling B; reject invalid pivot distance as a negative control. Require >=3 fresh deterministic reproductions for any violation.
- **Evidence grade / status / confidence:** E1 / ACTIVE / low confidence of exploit; **SIMULATION_NOT_EXECUTED** in this invocation. No observed canonical-history breach, no quantified financial impact, no confirmed-vulnerability report entry.
