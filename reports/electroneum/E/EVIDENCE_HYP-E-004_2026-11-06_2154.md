## Attacker route, controls, counterexample
A sender would have to fund a valid signed dynamic-fee transaction with actual effective gas price >=2^64, get it into a consensus-valid block, and find an economically consequential consumer of receipt fee metadata. Neither full-chain import nor a wrong external economic decision is shown. The strongest counterexamples are consensus/funding rejection, low-tip effective price, and canonical deposit credit based on transfer value rather than gas metadata. A positive linear reimbursement oracle from prior run showed **underpayment**, not attacker enrichment.
