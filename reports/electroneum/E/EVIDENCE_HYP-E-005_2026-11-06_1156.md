## Competing explanations and discriminating result
A. This does not establish that pruning, explicit rewind, or corruption can never remove them.
B. Ordinary reorg retains old header and RPC completes with a stale but internally consistent receipt: still **unexecuted**, belongs to HYP-E-003, now deferred.
C. Header disappears due to pruning/rewind/database failure: conceivable but no proof an R0 actor can cause this under production configuration; may be R3 hardening only.
